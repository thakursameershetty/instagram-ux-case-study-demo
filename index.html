Instagram Reels — The Accidental Refresh

00:00

Problem

Pulling down to reach the top can trigger a refresh. The current feed is replaced, and there is no way back.

People lose the reel they were watching, or one they meant to share.

Refreshed by accident?
Go back to your previous feed within 5 seconds
00:00

Solution

After a refresh, a snackbar offers Previous Feed for 5 seconds.

One tap restores the feed. Ignore it, and the new feed stays.
